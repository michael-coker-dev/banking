About the project

A thoughtful approach to financial app design.

Fund Grow is a banking-style interface prototype exploring how account dashboards, transaction history and everyday money-management workflows can be presented in a clear, approachable experience.

What this project demonstrates

The prototype brings together a responsive landing page, authentication screens, a dashboard, account and card views, statement history, and sample deposit and transfer interfaces. It demonstrates front-end development, interface structure and interaction design.

Technology

HTML, CSS, JavaScript ES modules, Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore integration.

Prototype notice: Fund Grow is not a bank or financial service. It does not accept deposits, move money, issue payment cards or provide investment services. Do not submit real financial or identity information.
