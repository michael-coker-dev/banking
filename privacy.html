Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

🔒 Your privacy is our priority. We never sell your personal data and we're committed to protecting your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, use our services, or contact our support team.

  • Personal identification information (name, email, date of birth)
  • Financial information (account details, transaction history)
  • Device and usage information (IP address, browser type)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our banking services
  • To process transactions and send confirmations
  • To detect and prevent fraud or security threats
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • To improve and personalize your experience

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption, secure socket layer (SSL) technology, and multi-factor authentication to protect your information.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with trusted third parties who assist in providing services, subject to strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze site traffic, and understand where our visitors come from.

6. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at privacy@fundgrow.com.
