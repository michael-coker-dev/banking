Frequently Asked Questions

How do I open an account?

Click the "Sign Up" button and fill out the registration form. It takes less than 3 minutes and you'll need a valid email address and government-issued ID.

Is my money safe with Fund Grow?

Yes, we use bank-grade encryption and partner with FDIC-insured institutions to protect your deposits up to applicable limits.

Are there any hidden fees?

No, we believe in complete transparency. All fees are disclosed upfront and there are never any surprise charges.

How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot password?" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email to create a new password.
